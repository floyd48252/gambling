Investigative Journalism

Uncovering hidden practices, following the evidence, and publishing the stories that deserve public attention.

Explore our reporting

We examine the systems, business practices, and decisions that shape betting markets and consumer experiences. Our work starts with evidence and gives readers the context to assess what it shows.

What this work includes

  • Reviewing public records, research, policies, and first-hand accounts.
  • Investigating advertising, consumer terms, safer-play tools, and market oversight.
  • Publishing sourced reporting that distinguishes documented facts from open questions.

Elevating lived experience and helping communities turn shared concerns into calls for reform and accountability.

Explore community work

People closest to an issue bring essential context. We help surface those perspectives and connect them with evidence that can inform public discussion and policy.

How we support advocacy
